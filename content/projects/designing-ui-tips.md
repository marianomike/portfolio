An introduction to cross-platform UI for beginners and UI artists who don't code, posted as a resource on the Roblox Developer Forum, where it drew over 120 likes.

## What it covers

- **Design mobile first** with the device emulator: if the UI fits a phone, it will likely fit everything else.
- **Scale over offset** for size, paired with aspect ratio and size constraints, and when offset is still the right call.
- **Anchor point and position**, and how they work together to keep elements where they belong.
- **Where UI goes**: common placement patterns, and working around Roblox's top bar, chat, leaderboard, mobile controls and the safe area.
