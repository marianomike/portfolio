Game of Thrones Slots Casino is a free to play licensed mobile slots game

As Principal UX Designer within the Casino/Match-3 org at Zynga, I was part of a central team allocated to help games ship. For my first project, I worked with the Game of Thrones Slots team for 6 months, helping define UI/UX best practices, standards and pipeline processes. I was also very hands on throughout feature development creating wireframes, visual designs, prototypes, and implementing in Unity.
