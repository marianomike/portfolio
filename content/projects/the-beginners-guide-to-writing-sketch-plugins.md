When I first started trying to write plugins for Sketch, I had a difficult time finding the right resources to get me going. There didn't seem to be any basic tutorials for Designers like me. So I wrote this article to provide that missing resource and help others find the right information to get started. It ended up being pretty well received by the Sketch community, shared by Sketchapp and Smashing Magazine and included in Sketch's own curated news. This seven part series starts with the basics of creating your first Sketch plugin, to more advanced data import and export.
