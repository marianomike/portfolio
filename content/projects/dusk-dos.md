Dusk Dos is Pusoy Dos as a roguelike, for iPhone and Android. You climb the Isles of Dusk one table at a time, choosing between two challengers at each step: creatures of Philippine folklore like the Kapre, the Adarna and the Mangkukulam, each bending one rule of the game. Every play scores points times mult, and the Embers you win buy crew cards from your village, combo levels and card changes in the shop before you face the island's boss. Lola teaches you the game in a minute, and the Lore Book fills in as you meet the cast. It's paid up front, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

The game was built in Unity with Claude Code. The look, the characters, the island maps and the tables came out of a concept lab I built alongside it: approve one look and it paints every asset in it.
