Pusoy Dos! is the Filipino card game for iPhone and Android. Play a quick match against three computer players, follow a journey through Philippine history where every table you clear earns a court card portrait of a real hero, or open a room and play friends online with a share code. It has no betting and no coins; you make progress by unlocking places, portraits, card backs and tables.

The game was built in Unity with Claude Code. The look, the court card paintings, the journey maps and the music came out of a concept lab I built alongside it.
