Stream Raiders is a unique game experience designed for Twitch Streamers to play with their viewers along side their main content.

As the Lead and only UI/UX Designer at the time, my responsibilities were wide. I mainly worked on the entire UI and UX of the flagship game Stream Raiders, which included visual design, Unity implementation, hookup, performance optimization and debugging. Since we were a small startup studio, I was also involved in the marketing and branding of our game and company.
