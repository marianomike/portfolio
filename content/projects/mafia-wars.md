Mafia Wars was one of the biggest Social Games on Facebook. At it's peak, Mafia Wars had over 3 million daily active users (DAU) and was earning close to 1 million dollars a day.

As Lead UI Designer on Mafia Wars, I was responsible to developing the look and feel of the interface, work closely with all disciplines (Game Design, Product Management, Engineering), plus help create features that drove revenue and retention.
